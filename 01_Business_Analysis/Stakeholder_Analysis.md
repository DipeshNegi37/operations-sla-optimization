# Stakeholder Analysis

## Project: Operations SLA & Turnaround Time Optimization

### Stakeholder Overview

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest | Influence | Key Needs | Engagement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Operations Head | Executive owner | High | High | Overall SLA/TAT performance and improvement priorities | Manage closely |
| Business Head | Business sponsor | High | High | Business impact, risks and expected benefits | Manage closely |
| Operations Manager | Process owner | High | High | Team performance, workload and bottlenecks | Manage closely |
| Team Lead | Operational SME | High | Medium | Root causes, queue issues and actionable interventions | Consult regularly |
| Operations Analyst | Data/process user | High | Medium | Detailed KPIs, trends and drill-down analysis | Consult regularly |
| Business Analyst | Project analyst | High | High | Requirements, analysis, documentation and recommendations | Lead |

## Stakeholder Needs

### Operations Head
- Executive-level SLA and TAT visibility.
- Identification of major performance drivers.
- Clear improvement priorities.
- Ability to monitor improvement after implementation.

### Business Head
- Understanding of business impact from SLA failures.
- Prioritized recommendations.
- Measurable expected outcomes.

### Operations Manager
- Team-level performance comparison.
- Workload and capacity visibility.
- Identification of high-risk request types.
- Root-cause information to support operational decisions.

### Team Lead
- Queue and processing bottlenecks.
- Rework and escalation drivers.
- Operational-level intervention opportunities.

### Operations Analyst
- Reliable KPI definitions.
- Detailed data for investigation.
- Consistent reporting logic.

### Business Analyst
- Clear business objectives.
- Agreed scope and requirements.
- Access to relevant operational data.
- Stakeholder validation of findings and recommendations.

## Communication Plan

| Stakeholder | Information | Frequency | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operations Head | Executive KPIs, key findings, recommendations | Monthly / milestone | Dashboard + summary |
| Business Head | Business impact and decisions required | Milestone | Presentation |
| Operations Manager | Team and process performance | Weekly | Dashboard / report |
| Team Lead | Operational issues and actions | Weekly | Working session |
| Operations Analyst | Data issues, definitions and analysis | As needed | Working session |

## RACI Summary

| Activity | Operations Head | Business Head | Operations Manager | Team Lead | Operations Analyst | Business Analyst |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Define business objective | A | A | C | C | I | R |
| Gather requirements | C | C | A | C | C | R |
| Validate KPI definitions | A | I | R | C | C | R |
| Data preparation | I | I | C | C | R | A |
| Data analysis | I | I | C | C | C | R/A |
| Validate findings | A | C | R | C | C | R |
| Approve recommendations | A | A | R | C | I | R |
| Monitor outcomes | A | C | R | R | C | R |

**R = Responsible, A = Accountable, C = Consulted, I = Informed**
