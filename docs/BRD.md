# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Operations SLA & Turnaround Time Optimization

**Company:** NexaVerify (fictional B2B operations company for this portfolio case study)

## Business Context
NexaVerify processes business verification and service requests for clients. Operations must complete requests within defined Service Level Agreements (SLAs). Management has observed increasing turnaround time (TAT) and declining SLA compliance, but current reporting does not clearly explain the operational drivers.

## Problem Statement
Operational performance is declining, with requests taking longer to complete and a growing proportion missing SLA. Management lacks a consolidated view of workload, queue time, processing time, rework, request type and team performance.

## Business Objective
Identify the primary drivers of high TAT and SLA breaches and provide data-driven recommendations to improve SLA compliance, reduce avoidable delays and improve operational capacity.

## Key Business Questions
1. What is overall SLA compliance?
2. How does SLA performance change over time?
3. What are average, P70 and P90 TAT?
4. Which request types have the highest TAT/breach rates?
5. Which teams have the strongest/weakest performance?
6. How does workload affect queue time and SLA compliance?
7. What is the impact of rework?
8. Which factors are associated with SLA breaches?
9. Where are the main bottlenecks?
10. What should management prioritize?

## Scope
**In:** request volume, SLA, TAT, queue/processing time, request category, priority, team/agent workload, rework, escalations, root-cause analysis, KPI framework, dashboard and recommendations.

**Out:** revenue, marketing, HR, customer acquisition, pricing and accounting.

## Success Criteria
- Clear KPI monitoring for SLA and TAT.
- Identification of high-risk request types and operational areas.
- Quantified contribution of queue time, processing time and rework.
- Evidence-based root causes.
- Prioritized recommendations with measurable targets.

## Core KPIs
SLA Compliance %, SLA Breach %, Average TAT, P70 TAT, P90 TAT, Queue Time, Processing Time, Rework Rate, Escalation Rate, Requests per Agent.

## Initial Hypotheses
- Higher workload may increase queue time and reduce SLA compliance.
- Reworked requests may have higher TAT.
- Some request types may contribute disproportionately to breaches.
- Team performance may vary materially.
- Queue time may be a major contributor to total TAT.

## Assumptions & Constraints
The dataset is synthetic and created for portfolio demonstration. No real customer/company data is used. Findings must be supported by analysis rather than treated as predetermined conclusions.

## Deliverables
BRD, stakeholder analysis, requirements, process maps, synthetic dataset, data dictionary, SQL/Python analysis, KPI framework, dashboard, root-cause analysis, recommendations, business-impact assessment and final portfolio case study.
