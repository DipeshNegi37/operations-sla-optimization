# Stakeholder Analysis

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Key Need | Engagement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Operations Head | High | High | Executive SLA/TAT visibility and priorities | Manage closely |
| Business Head | High | High | Business impact and recommendations | Manage closely |
| Operations Manager | High | High | Team performance, workload and bottlenecks | Manage closely |
| Team Lead | High | Medium | Operational root causes and interventions | Consult regularly |
| Operations Analyst | High | Medium | Detailed KPIs and analysis | Consult regularly |
| Business Analyst | High | High | Requirements, analysis and decision support | Lead |

## Stakeholder Needs
- **Operations Head:** overall KPIs, drivers, priorities and post-improvement monitoring.
- **Business Head:** business impact, risks and prioritized actions.
- **Operations Manager:** team comparison, workload and high-risk request types.
- **Team Lead:** queue, processing, rework and escalation drivers.
- **Operations Analyst:** consistent KPI definitions and reliable analysis.
- **Business Analyst:** clear objectives, scope, data and stakeholder validation.

## RACI Summary
| Activity | Operations Head | Business Head | Operations Manager | Team Lead | Operations Analyst | Business Analyst |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Define objective | A | A | C | C | I | R |
| Gather requirements | C | C | A | C | C | R |
| Validate KPIs | A | I | R | C | C | R |
| Prepare data | I | I | C | C | R | A |
| Analyze data | I | I | C | C | C | R/A |
| Validate findings | A | C | R | C | C | R |
| Approve recommendations | A | A | R | C | I | R |
| Monitor outcomes | A | C | R | R | C | R |

**R = Responsible, A = Accountable, C = Consulted, I = Informed**
